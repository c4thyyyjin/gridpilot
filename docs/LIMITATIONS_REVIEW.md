# Limitations review — original findings and current status

The table below preserves the original findings. In this version, items 1–3 have
been addressed within their documented scope. Item **4 is now implemented**:
per-seed metrics, complete feasibility diagnostics, worst-case counts, paired
95% intervals, and propagation of unexpected errors. See `FIX2_REVIEW.md`,
`FIX3_REVIEW.md`, and `FIX4_REVIEW.md`. Items **9 and 10 are now addressed** by an import-safe runner, explicit
outputs/configuration, guaranteed restoration, a tested dependency lock, current
documentation, and separated/grouped Pareto labels (see `FIX9_10_REVIEW.md`).
Item **8 now has grid-cap, capital-cost and demand-charge sensitivity analyses**
(see `FIX8_REVIEW.md`); real-site calibration and demand-aware optimization remain
outside that screen. For **5–7**, configurable online/delayed-information and ramp/overhead variants
plus expanded unscreened held-out synthetic validation are now implemented
(see `FIX5_7_REVIEW.md`). The held-out study exposes online/deferred-information feasibility failures;
controller robustness is not fixed. Per-resume/GPU granularity, within-hour
hardware behavior and real operational validation remain open; statistical intervals do not replace operational validation.
